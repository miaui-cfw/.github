# 🐱 MiaUI: The GPU-Powered FW for RG35XX legacy models!

Welcome to **MiaUI**! Based on the brilliantly minimalist [MinUI](https://github.com/shauninman/MinUI-Legacy-RG35XX), MiaUI takes the Anbernic legacy RG35XX experience to the next level by unlocking the true potential of the console's hardware. 

We love the clean, distraction-free philosophy of [MinUI](https://github.com/shauninman/MinUI-Legacy-RG35XX), but we also wanted more power under the hood. We integrated **Hardware GL Rendering** and native **GPU Scaling**, MiaUI breaks the ceiling of what this handheld can do—bringing advanced 3D systems.

## 🤝 Get Involved!

MiaUI is built by retro lovers, for retro lovers, and we are just getting started. Whether you are a C coder, a UI designer, or someone who loves compiling source ports, there is a place for you here. 

Since we rely on a custom GPU-enabled architecture, we are actively porting and adapting new games and cores. If you want to contribute:
1. **Fork our repos** in the `miaui-cfw` organization.
2. **Check the issues tab** for requested ports or core updates.
3. **Submit a Pull Request!** We review all community contributions and are always happy to help you get your code running on the RG35XX.

Grab your console, [flash the latest](https://github.com/miaui-cfw/MiaUI-GPU-RG35XX/releases) `.img`, and let's push this hardware to the limit.
